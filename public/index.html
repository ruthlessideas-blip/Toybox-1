Console Script — paste into F12 → Console on your Toybox upload page

How to use

  1. Open your Toybox upload page in the browser.
  2. Press F12 (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Option+I (Mac) to open DevTools.
  3. Click the Console tab.
  4. Paste the script above and press Enter.
  5. Watch the fields fill automatically. Check the console output for a summary of what was filled vs. what needs manual attention.
